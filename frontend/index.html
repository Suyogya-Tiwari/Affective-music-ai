How to Make Music 🎵

Step 1: Select a Core Emotion and Lead Instrument. This sets the overall vibe.

Step 2: Act as the Producer! Override the AI by picking a specific Chord Progression (e.g. Pop, Jazz) and a Drum Groove.

Step 3: Draw a starting melody on the Virtual Piano. The AI Co-Pilot will read your notes and finish the song for you!

Step 4: Click Generate. Watch the AI plot its mathematical decisions on the Piano Roll, then use the Studio Mixer to add Reverb and Low-Pass Filters in real-time.
